# rxagc cfo: 2000 frames per point of 1000 octets, a new PSDU per frame, clock offset
# uniform in ±40 ppm (CFO and sample clock, carrier 5.8 GHz), channel::FastAttackAgc (power
# over 16 samples, lock left on a large overload, 2 detector clocks per sample, lock level
# −10 dBFS, largest gain 73 dB), the gain held from the first lock inside the packet
# (assumed; `hold` has rows that hold it later), phase jump ±30° per gain change (assumed), 12-bit ADC.
# Chains on the ADC's samples:
#   float     = float DC blocker (pole 2^-7), float front, rx_ofdm (8 bits, clip 3);
#   int O/s   = dc_block_fixed (pole 2^-7), RxFrontFixed, correct_fixed to O bits,
#               decode_ppdu_fixed with in_bits O and in_shift s, tracker started from the
#               CFO word and the register fs/fc (FixedTracking::from_cfo_word): no float
#               between the ADC and the PSDU; the int columns of a row share one acquisition;
#   stream    = where a command has it: the receiver of ADR 0008 in a closed loop with the
#               gain control, a sample at a time (the column `front alone` of `loop`): the
#               streaming front (RxHandoffFixed), its own hold in place of the assumed one,
#               the offset of the two long training symbols removed, 13 bits, the decoder
#               at its defaults reading the beats; the front searches all through and the
#               frame is received if any stream decodes to it; its phase jumps are its own draws;
#   known     = the same chain behind a gain that knows the frame (it at -12 dBFS at
#               the ADC, no gain control, no DC offset);
#   raw       = the decoder handed the samples before the DC blocker.
# A frame's random stream depends on the row and the frame's number only.
# Noise at -30 dBFS at the largest gain, no DC offset. The clock offset's size uniform in
# the band of the row (40 ppm: 232 kHz of CFO, 0.74 of the subcarrier spacing), either sign.
# The rows of a point are the same frames but for the clock offset.
#   2^-7 = the decoder handed the blocker's output (the chain as it is);
#   ltf  = the decoder handed the samples before the blocker less the offset estimated
#          from the two long training symbols (ltf_dc_offset, ltf_dc_offset_fixed).
# Each cell: frames lost (not decoded to the PSDU), then +a−b: lost by this column alone and
# by the column 'float raw' alone (the same frames). A lost frame is one not acquired, acquired
# wrongly (the first acquisition of the stream is the only one tried) or not decoded.
# level: signal and noise of the frame's span at the settled gain, before the ADC clips and
# without the DC offset, dBFS, the mean of the powers over the frames. chg: gain changes per
# frame up to the hold (with no lock inside the packet: all of the stream's). held: frames in
# which the gain would have changed again before the packet's end without the hold.
