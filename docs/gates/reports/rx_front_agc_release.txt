# rxagc loop: ADR 0008, decision 2, as a closed loop a sample at a time. 2000 frames of 1000 octets per row, the
# frames of `hold` (the same scenes); noise at -30 dBFS at the largest gain unless the row says, no DC offset unless the
# row says; clock offset uniform in ±40 ppm, carrier 5.8 GHz. Gain control: the AD9361's reset values with 16
# samples of power and the overload exit on (as every command), stepped with the receiver (FastAttackAgc::start).
# Receiver, all in integers, in every column: each ADC sample through the streaming front (RxHandoffFixed: 8 beats
# a period, the replay 20 periods after the declaration), the decoder as its reader: it sees the beats alone, gives
# SIGNAL's verdict (decode_signal_fixed on the stream's first 208 samples, with the stream's tag) 50 periods
# after the beat that completes them and decodes a stream (decode_ppdu_fixed, the tracker started from the CFO
# word) when its end mark comes. The front searches all through; a frame is received if any stream decodes to it.
# Phase jump per gain change uniform in ±30°.
# Who keeps the gain still:
#   from the lock    = held from the control's first lock inside the packet to the stream's end
#                      (the tool knows where the packet is: the assumption of the other commands' columns);
#   timer + front    = the control keeps every lock 512 samples, and the front's own hold (RxHandoffFixed::hold:
#                      from the declaration to the end SIGNAL's length gives, to the verdict when SIGNAL fails,
#                      to the longest PPDU's end with no verdict) holds the control from the sample after;
#   timer<max + front = the same, the timer keeping only a lock taken below the largest gain
#                      (a lock on noise is left at the first overload);
#   front alone      = the front's hold without any timer and without the release (decision 2 before 2026-11-22);
#   timer<max alone  = that timer and no hold;   nothing = neither;
#   t<max + front +40/+400 = timer<max + front with the hold acting 40 and 400 samples (2 and 20 µs) later, at
#                      both its edges: the control stays held that long past the hold's end;
#   front + release  = decision 2 as amended 2026-11-22, the model's default: front alone, and the front
#                      gives up its hold when 2 of the last 4 ADC samples sit on a rail
#                      (RxHandoffFixed; the stream goes on, only a new declaration raises the hold again);
#   front + release +40 = the same with the hold acting 40 samples (2 µs) later, its release with it.
# The release is off in every column but the last two.
# A released control returns to the largest gain and searches (an assumption).
# Rows 'preamble +': the frame's two training fields alone (320 samples, nothing after them)
# are sent first, 280 to 330 samples before the frame: an acquisition whose SIGNAL is noise.
# Rows 'frame + SIFS +': a whole frame of 100 octets at the same rate and level first, the
# frame counted 320 samples after its end.
# Rows 'noise -5': the noise at -5 dBFS at the largest gain, above the lock level: the
# control locks on it below the largest gain.
# Rows 'DC per index -20': an offset of -20 dBFS at every gain, its direction per gain index; rows 'DC before
# gain -10': an offset of -10 dBFS at the largest gain that falls with the gain, one direction (channel::GainDc).
# Rows 'under -D dB': a weaker frame of the same rate and length, D dB below the frame, is being received
# (from 600 to 650 samples into the stream) when the frame begins 1200 samples into it; the frame is the
# one counted, and its SNR is the row's.
# Rows 'hit L +D dB': white noise added to the frame over L samples, 1000 samples into it, nominally D dB above
# the frame's mean power (one draw: a few dB either way at L = 4), before the channel's delay and clock offset.
# Each cell: frames lost, +a−b against 'from the lock' (lost by this column alone, by that one

#   2/4; slow: X     = 2 of 4, until the decoder's verdict on SIGNAL of the open stream names 6, 9 or
